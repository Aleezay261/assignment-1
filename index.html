About the Drama Club

The Drama Club is a student group at Qarshi University for people who like acting, writing and doing stage work. It's a place to try something new and make friends that match your energy and build confidence in front of audience

Everyone is welcome, even if you have never been on stage before. You don't need any experience to join

Join us

What we do

  • Weekly rehearsals where we will practise scenes and games
  • Two stage plays every semester for the university
  • Acting and script writing gathering
  • Backstage work like lights, costumes and sets

Our values

  • Everyone is welcomed, whatever their experience
  • We respect and support each other
  • We learn by trying and mistakes are fine
  • We work as a team on stage and backstage
